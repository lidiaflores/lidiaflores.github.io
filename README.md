<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"small","fontFamily":"system-font"} -->
<p class="has-system-font-font-family has-small-font-size has-custom-font" style="font-family:system-font">I am a 6th year Ph.D candidate in Informatics at UC Irvine's School of Computer Science with a 3.9 GPA where I am co-advised by <a href="https://www.informatics.uci.edu/explore/faculty-profiles/madhu-reddy/">Madhu Reddy</a> and <a href="https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/schuellerlab/stephen-schueller-phd/">Stephen Schueller</a>. I'm also a <a href="https://research.google/programs-and-events/phd-fellowship/recipients/"><strong>Google PhD Research Fellow</strong></a> in Health &amp; Bioscience where I am mentored by <a href="https://health.google/health-research/">Priya Gupta</a>. My research is focused on understanding the role of AI transparency in large language models on user health-related information-seeking behaviors. My research aims to understand how health chatbots may shape patient health outcomes, along with factors that may impact trust towards AI. My research also extends into evaluating the implementation of AI tools in medical settings and the ethical considerations of using AI for medical research. My scientific findings have been published in top journals such as the <strong><a href="https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamasurgery/article-abstract/2806778">Journal of American Medical Association (JAMA Surgery)</a></strong>.&nbsp;</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"small"} -->
<p class="has-small-font-size"> Before my PhD, I was a research associate at the <strong><a href="https://www.emergencymed.uci.edu/research-overview.asp">Emergency Medicine Department</a></strong> at UCI Health School of Medicine where I gained experience in collecting, analyzing, and visualizing big data to improve the timeliness of health data.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"fontSize":"small"} -->
<p class="has-small-font-size">As a researcher, I am a quick learner with a keen eye for detail and stellar computer skills. I have been awarded scholarships from <strong><a href="https://about.google/">Google</a></strong>, UCI's Startup Fund Award, Graduate Recruitment Fellowship, <a href="https://inclusion.uci.edu/education-events/mentoring/latino-excellence-and-achievement-award/">UCI LEAD</a>, UC Irvine Dissertation Writing Fellowship, and UC Irvine Staff Assembly Scholarship.</p>
